**Overview:**  
Co-authored a scientific paper titled *"An Artificial Intelligence Approach to Intrusion Detection in Smart Home IoT Environments"*, published in the Springer series *Computational Intelligence in Engineering Science (ICCIES 2026)*.

**What We Addressed:**  
Traditional network defense mechanisms struggle on resource-constrained IoT hardware. Our research introduced a self-adaptive Intrusion Detection System (IDS) based on a hybrid Edge–Cloud architecture.

**Key Contributions & Results:**
- **Edge Deployment:** Implemented a lightweight LightGBM model on a Raspberry Pi (gateway) for real-time anomaly detection with an ultra-low delay of ~8.4 ms.
- **Cloud Distillation:** Developed a cloud-based CNN–LSTM model that performs incremental learning on anomalous traffic and distills its knowledge back into the edge model.
- **Performance:** Achieved 96.94% accuracy and 97.01% F1-score on the CIC-IDS2017 dataset, while maintaining a minimal resource footprint (25–30% CPU, 300–400 MB RAM) on the edge device.
