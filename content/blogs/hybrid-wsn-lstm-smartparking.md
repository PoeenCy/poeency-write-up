**Overview:**  
This project is a comprehensive simulation of an energy-efficient IoT smart parking system. It combines Wireless Sensor Networks (WSN) optimization with a Bidirectional LSTM (Bi-LSTM) model for real-time parking availability prediction based on traffic generated by SUMO.

**What I did:**
- Designed and benchmarked a novel hybrid WSN routing protocol optimized by Genetic Algorithms (GA), comparing it against Direct and LEACH protocols.
- Integrated a TDMA scheduling mechanism to eliminate collisions and utilized an accurate path loss model for far-field scenarios.
- Deployed a Bi-LSTM neural network to predict real-time parking occupancy, effectively handling missing data from packet loss and temporal patterns.

**Key Achievements & Skills Gained:**  
- **Network Longevity:** Extended the WSN lifetime by 2.8x and reduced energy consumption by 73% compared to standard LEACH.
- **AI Integration:** Successfully bridged the gap between low-level network simulations and high-level AI forecasting.
- Skills applied: Python, SUMO, TensorFlow/Keras, and WSN topology design.
